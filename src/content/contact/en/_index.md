### How your one-of-a-kind piece is made

1. *Get inspired!*

   Browse our products. Since every piece is sewn individually by hand, only your imagination sets the limits for colours and patterns.
2. *Tell us what you dreamt up!*

   Add your wishes to the basket. Send us which products you like and which colour palette would suit the nursery.
3. *Secure payment.*

   Once everything is in the basket, you can choose between bank transfer in advance, online card payment and cash on delivery — whichever is most convenient for you.
4. *Your parcel is on its way!*

   We ship the carefully crafted products with GLS courier right to your door, keeping you informed along the way.
