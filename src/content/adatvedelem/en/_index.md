This privacy notice (hereinafter: the notice) explains what personal data we process while using babasarok.net, on what legal basis, for how long, and what your rights are under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

## 2. What data we process and why

### Orders and quote requests

When you place an order or request a quote on the "Checkout" page, you send us the following data: name, e-mail address, phone number (if you provide one), delivery address (if the chosen delivery method requires it), any message, and the contents of the basket (the selected products and their settings, together with the price calculation).

The legal basis of the processing is that it is necessary for entering into the contract and essential for performing the order (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### Visiting the site and contact

The contact page only shows our contact details; visiting the page in itself does not collect personal data. The content of your order in progress stays in your browser's local storage and only reaches us when you place the order.

### Measurement tools (only with your consent)

The site measures visits and order conversions. If you expressly consent, the following tools are loaded:

- **Google Analytics / Google Ads** (Google LLC) — measures visits and order conversions via Google Tag Manager.
- **Meta pixel** (Meta Platforms Inc.) — measures visits to the site and the order conversions.

The legal basis for the measurement is your consent. These tools store cookies on your device and forward the visitor's IP address and device data to the providers. Both providers are US companies, therefore the international transfer of the data is carried out on the basis of the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (SCC) and a data protection addendum (DPA) concluded with the providers. If you do not give your consent, the measurement tools are not loaded.

You can modify or withdraw your consent at any time with the "Change consent" button below; the withdrawal takes effect immediately (the measurements no longer start), and it does not affect the validity of the consent-based processing that took place before the withdrawal.

## 3. Cookies

Our own site does not use cookies for its operation. If you consent to the measurement tools, the following cookies may end up on your device:

- **Google cookies** (`_ga`, `_ga_*`, `_gcl_*`): set by Google, for at most 2 years; for analytics and ad-measurement purposes.
- **Meta cookies** (`_fbp`: 30 days; `_fbc`, `_fr`: at most 365 days): set by Meta, for ad-measurement purposes.

## 4. Where your data goes (processors)

- **Our own webshop system (Vendure)** — we store the orders and quote requests on our own self-hosted server (Hetzner, Germany); the confirmation e-mails go through Cloudflare's e-mail sending service.
- **Cloudflare** — the hosting and delivery of the site (CDN) and the sending of the order confirmation e-mails happen through it; the requests (e.g. the IP address) are processed under a data processing agreement with a short retention period.
- **Google LLC** and **Meta Platforms Inc.** — measurement tools, details in section 2.

## 5. How long we store the data

- **Orders and quote requests:** if the order is fulfilled, we keep it for 8 years (the mandatory accounting and tax record retention period) and delete it afterwards; if a quote does not lead to an order, we delete it after 12 months.
- **Consent:** stored in your browser and deleted immediately when you change your choice.
- **Measurement cookies:** for the periods listed in section 3, at the providers.
- **Cloudflare request data:** for a short period, according to Cloudflare's publicly available retention policy.

## 6. Your rights

You may request information about, the rectification, erasure or restriction of processing of your personal data; you may object to the processing, and you may also request data portability. We reply to requests by e-mail within 1 month. If you believe that the processing of your personal data violates applicable law, you may lodge a complaint with the National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information ([https://www.naih.hu/](https://www.naih.hu/)).

## 7. Modification of this notice

The data controller may modify this notice at any time in line with changes in the processing or the legal environment. The version in force is the notice published on this page.
